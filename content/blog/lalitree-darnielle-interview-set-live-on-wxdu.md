Recently, Durham musician, singer, and songwriter Lalitree Darnielle visited WXDU to chat with us and play some songs live in the studio. We're excited that the recording of this show is now available for streaming. Listen in as Lalitree and her band bring their unique mix of chamber instruments (violin, cello, and upright bass) and electronic/sampled beats to meet somewhere in an art-pop wonderland. Check out her record Forest Fires on any streaming service (link: [ffm.to/fofi](ffm.to/fofi)) and watch for a new EP, which she is recording now, to be released later this year.

Listen here: [https://wxdu.org/short/lalitree](https://wxdu.org/short/lalitree)
